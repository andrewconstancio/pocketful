How it works

With you from morning to night.

Pocketful checks in a few times a day, at times that fit your schedule. Each check-in takes seconds. No streak pressure and no endless feed.

  1. Morning

    Start with a thought

    A short line the AI picks for what you're working on, like “Be present where you are.” Read it, or say it out loud to help it stick.

  2. Through the day

    Check in with yourself

    Gentle nudges ask how you're doing and bring your thought back when you need it. Log how you feel in a tap, or add a quick note.

  3. Evening

    Reflect on your day

    See how your day went, from your morning thought to each check-in, and jot down whatever's on your mind.
